# Hero Siege Wiki feedback

This is the public correction and content-feedback tracker for
[Hero Siege Wiki](https://herosiegewiki.com). The website's source repository
and operational systems are private; this repository contains no site code,
analytics data, research archive, or deployment credentials.

## What to report here

Open a public issue when a wiki page is inaccurate, outdated, unclear, missing
important context, poorly translated, or has a broken link. Reports are welcome
in English, Spanish, Portuguese, or Russian.

Please include:

- the exact wiki page URL;
- what is wrong and what the page should say instead;
- the current Hero Siege version or season when relevant;
- a source link, screenshot, or reproducible in-game observation that supports
  the correction.

[Open a page-correction report](https://github.com/adshz/herosiegewiki-feedback/issues/new?template=page-correction.yml)

## What not to report here

This tracker is not operated by Panic Art Studios and cannot help with Hero
Siege accounts, purchases, bans, crashes, multiplayer problems, or other game
support. Use the [official Hero Siege channels](https://playherosiege.com/en) for
those requests.

For copyright or takedown requests, follow the process on the wiki's
[copyright page](https://herosiegewiki.com/copyright).

## Public-submission notice

Issues and attachments are visible to everyone. Do not post passwords, API
keys, account identifiers, email addresses, payment details, or other private
information. Reports are reviewed against available evidence before a wiki page
is changed; submitting an issue does not guarantee publication.
